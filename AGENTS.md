- Company segments live in public.company_segments (per org, managed list) referenced by companies.segment_id; companies.segment text is deprecated and must not be read or written — keeps segment names consistent and renameable.
- Inbound webhook and spreadsheet import resolve company+contact only through public.resolve_company_contact (import preview via preview_import_resolution); deals are written with company_id + lead_contacts, never legacy lead name/email/phone/company fields — one shared rule keeps both entry points consistent.
- companies.trade_name_source ('provided'|'placeholder') decides whether inbound data may replace the trade name: only placeholder names (from contact/e-mail/Instagram/'Sem nome') are replaced, and any manual edit sets 'provided' — protects real names from webhook overwrites.
- Companies/Contacts list screens read through list_companies / list_contacts (SECURITY INVOKER over *_overview views, security_invoker) with filters as jsonb; list state lives in the URL via useUrlListState — server-side paging/sorting and shareable links.
- WhatsApp → CRM (docs/WHATSAPP.md): conversas ligam a negócios só por contacts.phone_key → lead_contacts de negócio aberto, e a IA muda etapa só por server/negocios/etapa.ts (moverEtapa) — contato sem telefone ou sem lead_contacts fica invisível, e outro caminho pularia SLA/webhook da etapa.
- Inbound webhook on an existing deal (same company + funnel): only a won/inactive/archived deal re-enters the first stage; an open one keeps its stage and gets a note — a lead already talking to sales must not fall back to Novo Lead and re-trigger the Softeo bot.
